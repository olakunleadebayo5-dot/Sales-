# Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail product data to evaluate sales performance, revenue generation, and the impact of key business factors such as promotions, product placement, seasonality, and country of origin. The goal is to provide actionable insights that can support better decision-making in product strategy, marketing, and inventory planning.

---

 ## 📁 Dataset Summary
* Product ID and Name
* Product Category and Section (Men/Women)
* Product Position (Aisle, End-cap, Front of Store)
* Promotion Status (Yes/No)
* Season (Winter, Summer, etc.)
* Sales Volume and Price
* Country of Origin
